# Product

## What it is

An **AI-powered goal achievement platform**. Fitness & wellness is V1; the core
is domain-neutral (spec §4). It is **not** primarily a fitness/habit tracker or
a chatbot — those are surfaces over an accountability engine.

## Core promise (spec §5)

Help a customer define a meaningful goal, understand why it matters, break it
into actions, commit, act, be held accountable, track progress, surface
barriers, learn from misses, adapt, and build discipline through repetition.

## The behavioral loop (spec §6)

`UNDERSTAND → PLAN → COMMIT → ACT → CHECK → LEARN → ADAPT → REPEAT → DISCIPLINE → GOAL`

## Three non-negotiable capabilities (spec §7)

1. AI accountability 2. Progress tracking 3. AI coaching.

## Transformation (spec §8)

From *"I have a goal but can't follow through"* to *"I understand what stops
me, I have a plan I can execute, I'm held accountable, and I'm building the
discipline to act consistently."*

## Accountability behavior

- The AI **recommends → discusses → the customer commits** (spec §10). It is a
  knowledgeable partner, not an authoritarian task manager.
- **Adaptive** style (supportive … firm); the system learns what works per
  customer (spec §11).
- **Escalation** levels 1–5 (support → investigate → challenge → redesign →
  escalate); never escalates on a single miss; challenges only with evidence
  (spec §12, §13).
- **Evidence-based** observations with explicit source-of-truth tiers; never
  presents a guess as a fact or shames the customer (spec §15, §16, §27).

## Personalized home (spec §33)

Answers "what do I need to do today?" in seconds: today's action, current
commitment, the partner's message, progress, one behavioral insight (tier
labeled) — not a wall of charts.

## North-star metric (spec §51)

**Customers consistently taking meaningful action toward their goals** — not
messages, screen views, or time-in-app.

## Safety

General wellness coaching, explicitly non-clinical; recommends professional
support when a situation exceeds safe scope (spec §28).
